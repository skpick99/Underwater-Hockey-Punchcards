Subject: Update on San Diego Hockey Club Session Pricing

Dear San Diego Hockey Club Members,

Over the past few years, the costs of running our club have continued to rise. To help cover these expenses while keeping our sessions affordable, we’ll be making a small adjustment to our pricing starting October 1st.

Here’s what’s changing:

Punchcards will remain $100, but will cover 10 plays instead of 11.

Single-session payments will increase to $12 per play.

Important notes about punchcards:

Any punchcard purchased before October 1st will still be valid for 11 plays, no matter when you use it.

Punchcards purchased on or after October 1st will be valid for 10 plays.

The punchcard system will automatically track your usage and reflect your balance in your game-day emails.

If you’d like to lock in an 11-play punchcard, you’re welcome to purchase one during September—even if you still have games remaining on your current card. The system will use up your current card first before activating the new one.

We appreciate your understanding and continued support of the club. These changes will help us keep the San Diego Hockey Club running strong while maintaining the quality of play we all enjoy.

Thank you,
