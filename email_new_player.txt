Welcome to the Underwater Hockey community! We are thrilled to have you join us and 
excited for you to experience this unique sport.

Here is everything you need to know to get started:

RSVP before the game
	Platform: 
		We use Meetup to manage our schedule.
	Action:
		Join our group and RSVP for games here: San Diego Underwater Hockey Club  
		meetup.com/san-diego-underwater-hockey-meetup/
	Why: 
		This helps us balance teams in advance and track player attendance.

Pool Costs & Punchcards
	We have a virtual punchcard system that reads Meetup signups for team creation and billing.

	Your First 5 Games are Free: 
		We want you to try the sport risk-free! We have created a 
		complimentary 5-game Punchcard for you, and it's now active.
		Watch for an email each time you play a game.
	Regular Pricing after your free games: 
		We split pool rental costs using a digital "Punchcard" system. 
		A punchcard costs $100 for 8 games ($12.50 per game)

Tracking Your Attendance
	Automated Alerts: 
		Our system automatically deducts a punch each time you play. You will
		receive an email update showing your games used and remaining balances.
	Whitelisting: 
		Please add sandiegouwh@gmail.com to your email contacts. This ensures
		our updates and punchcard receipts do not get lost in your spam folder.

Welcome aboard, and we look forward to seeing you at the pool!
